1. Business objectives

Phase 2 showcased a repeatable problem: El Paso / Juarez residents missing out on events due to scattered listings across multiple platforms. GPI – Gracias Por Invitar will help solve this problem by providing centralized event discovery across both sides of the border, to give event-goers advance notice to plan and attend.

  • Centralize events. Create a centralized platform for event promoters to publish events, removing the need for users to scatter across multiple platforms to stay informed.
  • Monetize on event publications. Offer paid featured or promoted listings to venues and organizers, an extra channel to reach users and a source of recurring platform revenue.
  • Promote marketing on events. Give organizers and small businesses a low-effort way to market their events.

2. Scope boundary

In scope

  • A centralized event calendar
  • An event feed listed by date (upcoming events)
  • A feed personalized by interests and preferences
  • Marketing of events on the platform
  • Monetization of published events

Out of scope

  • Ticketing and payment processing

3. Constraints

  • Fixed semester timeline. The project is built through sprints, with no budget for sponsorship or paid licenses.
  • Uneven data availability. Juarez and El Paso event information may be inconsistent or inaccurate, so analytics on the data may be limited.
  • Event planners. The project depends on the availability and accuracy of local event information supplied by event planners, which may vary across Juarez and El Paso.

4. Assumptions

These assumptions are based on what the team heard in the Phase 2 interviews.

  • Event organizers and venues will be willing to post on the platform instead of relying only on social media, and to update published events if any information changes.
  • Users will be willing to make an account and set their interests so the event feed can be personalized.

5. Success criteria

CriterionTarget or measure
Event listing for organizersAt least 20 Juarez and El Paso organizers post events by the end of the pilot run
Advance notice for usersAt least 80% of events are shown to users one week before the event
Interest in paid featured listingsAt least 2 event organizers express interest in paying for a featured listing during the pilot run
