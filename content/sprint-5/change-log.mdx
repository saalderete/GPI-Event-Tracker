## What this log is

Section 8 of the course guidelines asks every sprint page after Sprint 1 for a change log: what was revised from the prior sprint's version, why, and what downstream pages or documents the change affected. This is Sprint 5's log. It covers 3 to 16 November 2026. No Sprint 1, 2, 3 or 4 document was revised during it; the sprint's four public documents are new, and the portal itself changed only to carry them.

Each entry names what changed, the reason the record gives for it, and what it touched. The record line of each entry links the history of the file in the [portal's repository](https://github.com/saalderete/GPI-Event-Tracker-), where every change to it can be read.

## The earlier documents, carried

The Crisis Response Memo works from the earlier documents and changes none of them. Its decisions and its recommendation answer the course's scenario, not the project's own plan, so the [Project Charter](/sprint-1/project-charter/), the [Project Budget](/sprint-2/budget/), the [Service Level Indicators](/sprint-4/service-level-indicators/) and the [Error Budget and Policy](/sprint-4/error-budget-policy/) stay at their current versions. The three changes the Sprint 3 change note proposed to the [Business Strategy](/sprint-1/business-strategy/) still wait on its owner.
