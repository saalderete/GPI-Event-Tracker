## What this document is

The event site's wireframes: its screens in outline, with a crossed box wherever a picture goes and the site's own words for the copy, drawn in Lucidchart and exported one board a page. A map of the screens and how they reach each other comes first, then each desktop screen with numbered notes beside it, then the phone. They were drawn on 3 November 2026 from the event site as it then stood at gpi-j4aw.onrender.com, by Claude through Claude Code at the portal lead's direction, as the sprint's disclosure records. The PDF is the document; this page is its cover.
