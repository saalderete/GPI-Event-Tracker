## What this document is

The event site's high-fidelity mockups, in the file's own words "every screen of the site at full fidelity, at 1440 and at 390 wide, in the order of the wireframes: real type, colour, pictures and listings." Four boards come first, then the desktop screens with numbered notes beside them, then the phone screens. They are stills of the live site at graciasporinvitarelpaso.com, taken on 11 November 2026 with motion paused and the clock set to the next morning, Thursday 12 November, so every screen shows the same day. The listings are the site's own, from organizers' public pages, and the pictures are the site's generated illustrations. They were made by Claude through Claude Code at the portal lead's direction, as the sprint's disclosure records. The PDF is the document; this page is its cover.

## What it contains

Three parts, as the cover lists them:

1. **Start here.** From wireframe to mockup, the wireframes' frames beside the mockup's with what carried over and what changed; Colour, the nine palettes the visitor chooses from; Type, the two families; and Components, the pieces every screen is built from, with their states.
2. **Desktop, 1440.** Home in two boards, Calendar, Event page, Saved, For organizers, Post an event and Interests, each page naming the wireframe page it follows.
3. **Phone, 390.** Home in two boards, the Menu, Calendar, Event page, Saved, For organizers, Post an event and Interests, on two pages.
