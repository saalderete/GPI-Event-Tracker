## What this document is

The event site's mockups: each screen of the [wireframes](/sprint-5/wireframes/) in the site's colour and type, with a grey box and a cross wherever a picture goes and the wireframes' sample content in place of real listings. A style page comes first, then the seven desktop screens with numbered notes beside them, then the phone. They were drawn from the wireframes on 16 November 2026 by Claude through Claude Code at the portal lead's direction, as the sprint's disclosure records, and they replace the first version of 11 November, which was stills of the live site; the [change log](/sprint-5/change-log/) says why. The PDF is the document; this page is its cover.

## What it contains

Three parts, as the cover lists them:

1. **Style.** The default palette, Clinic White, with each colour's value; the two families, Jura and Inter, at the sizes the screens use; and the components every screen is built from: buttons, chips and tags, fields, the listing row and the placeholder for a picture.
2. **Desktop, 1440.** Home, Calendar, Event page, Saved, For organizers, Post an event and Interests, each naming the wireframe page it follows.
3. **Phone, 390.** Home with the Menu open, and the Calendar.
