## What this document is

Team 9's memo to the CEO on the sprint's crisis exercise: one incident that sets off three emergencies at once, answered with a triage, a decision log, a governance response, the reliability impact, new risk entries, a personnel transition plan and one recommendation. The incident is the course's scenario, not something that happened on the event site: the provider's update, the pilot and its users, the organizer's email, the forum post, the lead developer's resignation and the hiring freeze come from the exercise, and the memo says where it fitted them to the project, as when it casts Render, the site's host, as the provider. What the memo measures them against is the team's own: the charter, the budget, the service levels and the error budget published on this portal. This is the team's file of 12 November 2026, signed by all five members, in the six-page version made that evening. The PDF is the document; this page is its cover.

## What it contains

Seven sections, in the memo's own headings:

1. **Situation and Triage.** What happened, the immediate consequences, the triage order of the three emergencies, and a check of each against the risk lists the team had.
2. **Decision Log.** Six decisions, D1 to D6, each with its time, its emergency, the options rejected, the reasoning, the owner and whether it can be reversed; a later decision revises an earlier one in a new row.
3. **Governance Response.** The first version of the team's governance response, written after the incident: the incident owner, the risk classification under the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, the bias monitoring plan and the transparency response.
4. **Delivery and Reliability Impact.** Which service level was breached, the error budget and the action it requires, the dashboard metrics, and what would have caught the problem first.
5. **Updated Risk Register Entries.** Three entries in the format of the budget's Step 9 risk table, each with an owner, an early warning signal, a trigger response and its last response validation.
6. **Personnel Transition Plan.** Knowledge transfer, the ten-business-day handoff, the reassignment of responsibilities, cognitive load and skills gaps, and the scope adjustment.
7. **CEO Briefing.** Five key messages, what the team will not say, and the executive recommendation.

Then the signatures, Appendix A, the response log of 12 November, and Appendix B, the team's note on its use of AI.
