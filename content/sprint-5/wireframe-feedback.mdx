## What this document is

Team 9's one-page write-up of the feedback on the event site's [wireframes](/sprint-5/wireframes/). In its own words, the team "demoed our wireframes of the G.P.I event site to other teams in class on November 5th, and wrote down every comment." It records what each comment told the team and what changed in the [mockups](/sprint-5/mockups/) or what the team concluded. This is the team's file of 16 November 2026, as delivered. The PDF is the document; this page is its cover.
