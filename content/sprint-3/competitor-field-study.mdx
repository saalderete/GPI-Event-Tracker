## What this document is

The Session 13 field week sent every team out of the classroom to test its competitor assumptions against what real people do: three guided interviews of about 15 minutes with people outside the course, at least one of them a former user of a competing solution; a 30-minute teardown of each competitor profiled, on the team's own account; and four team deliverables built from that evidence. This is Team 9's file for the event-goer slice, submitted on 19 October 2026 as the team wrote it. The PDF is the document; this page is its cover.

## What it contains

The file opens with the field week requirements checklist (slides 21 and 27), each item marked against the evidence log, and then carries the four team deliverables the session asks for, in the document's own words:

1. **Evidence log.** "One row per interview or teardown. Tiers: 1 = first-hand behavior (our interviews, our teardowns); 2 = public artifacts (cite URL and access date)."
2. **Competitive Landscape.** The competitor map, "three columns, one per competitor type," with the status quo in its own column; the comparison table, "one column per competitor profiled"; and the switching triggers, ranked "now that all interviews are in (1 = strongest across interviews)."
3. **Change note.** "What changed in the business case, and why. One row per change, each tied to evidence IDs." The rationale column is written by the team.
4. **Candidate risk entries.** "1 to 3 risks for the Sprint 3 risk register," each with a trigger, a proposed owner, a mitigation and its evidence IDs.

Interviewees appear by code only, never by name, and every claim in the file carries the ID of the evidence it rests on. The individual deliverables of the same week, each member's field notes and one-page contradiction memo, are submitted separately through Blackboard and are not on the portal.
