## Business objectives

Phase 2 showcased a repeatable problem: El Paso / Juarez residents missing out on events due to scattered listing across multiple platforms. GPI – Gracias Por Invitar will help solve this problem by providing a centralized event discovery across both sides of the border to give event goers advance notice to plan and attend.

- **Centralize events.** Create a centralized platform for event promoters to publish events, removing the need for users to scatter across multiple platforms to stay informed.
- **Monetize on event publications.** Offer paid featured/promoted listings to venues and organizers, offering an extra channel to reach users and generate recurring platform revenue.
- **Promote marketing on events.** Give organizers and small businesses a low effort way to market their events.
