## What this document is

The team's sketches of the product's screens, drawn by hand and scanned, one screen a page. This is Team 9's file, submitted on 2 November 2026 as the team drew it. The PDF is the document; this page is its cover.

## What it contains

Four screens, each under its own heading in the file:

1. **Hero section.** The landing: the name and the page links across the top with Post an Event at the right, a numbered chapter rail down the left, the mountains and the city behind, the line "Everything happening, before it happens." and an Open Calendar button.
2. **Creating event section.** The "Post an Event" form: event name, a category dropdown, the date, starts and ends, the venue, "Who is it for" with an example, a Free toggle with a cost field and a currency, and "Know before you go" as a text area, with a flyer preview beside the form that carries the category, the price, the event name, the date and the location.
3. **Interests.** "What are you into?": a line on how the calendar leads with what is picked, and eight tiles to pick, Music, Markets, Car meets, Food, Family, Sports, Arts and Nightlife.
4. **Event feed.** The listings by day, Today, Tomorrow and the date after, with filters across the top and a heart on each row to save it.
