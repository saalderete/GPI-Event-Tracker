## What this document is

The second half of Sprint 4's service levels work: what the team does when a target is missed. The file takes the two objectives from the [Service Level Indicators](/sprint-4/service-level-indicators/), expresses each as an error budget of allowed misses, writes the policy for when a budget is spent, sets the escalation path and this sprint's incident roles, and reviews one real problem from the earlier sprints without blame. This is Team 9's file, dated 2 November 2026, as the team wrote it. The PDF is the document; this page is its cover.

## What it contains

The file has five parts, in the document's own words:

1. **Targets.** Each indicator with its target, its window and who measures it.
2. **Budgets.** For each objective, the budget as a count, the "policy action" when it is spent, its owner, and "who we tell, and when."
3. **Escalation.** Three severity levels, each with an example on the portal, when fixing starts and when the instructor is told.
4. **Incident roles.** The person holding each role this sprint.
5. **Incident review.** A blameless review of a problem from Sprint 3, in which the same evidence ID pointed to different evidence in two submitted documents: its summary, its impact, its timeline from 12 October to 2 November 2026, and its contributing causes, each a gap in the process rather than in a person.
