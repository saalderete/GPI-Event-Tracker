## 2. Budgets

| Field | Objective 1 | Objective 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Objective** | 95% of the event details should be accurate and up to date, this will be ensured | 99% of the time the site should be up and running every sprint (2 weeks). |
| **Budget** | 5% of 20 verified event listings = 1 inaccurate listing per week | 1% of 336 hours ~ 3 hours and 20 minutes of downtime per sprint |
| **Policy Action** | If budget is spent, halt event listing. Correct every incorrect listing and double the audit sample to 40 for the next week | If the budget is spent, freeze feature implementations for the rest of the sprint. Fix the issue and write a note of the issue and fix |
| **Owner** | Emmanuel, Oscar : Own the fixing through curation | Samuel – Site manager |
| **Who we tell, and when** | The team in the weekly meetings. The instructor if the budget is spent two weeks in a row. | The team immediately in the group chat. The instructor if the budget is spent |
