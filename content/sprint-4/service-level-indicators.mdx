## What this document is

Sprint 4 opens the course's performance block, and its first team deliverable says what "working" means for the product: two service level indicators, each with a precise definition, a measurement tool, a schedule, an owner and a target. This is Team 9's file, dated 2 November 2026, as the team wrote it. The PDF is the document; this page is its cover.

## What it contains

The file has four parts, in the document's own words:

1. **SLI specification.** For each indicator, a "definition (one precise sentence)", the measurement tool, schedule and owner, the SLO "target and time window", the rules it follows, and who would notice a miss.
2. **Measurement plan.** A method "repeatable by any teammate" for each indicator, with its owner, its schedule and where the results are logged.
3. **Five-rule check on our targets.** Each SLO held against the five rules: don't copy today's numbers, keep it simple, avoid absolutes, as few SLOs as possible, perfection can wait.
4. **Share-out tests.** Whether any teammate would get the same number, whether each target is realistic and what it costs, and what the team changed as a result: a written "Good to go" checklist for the listing audit, and an automated uptime monitor checking every 5 minutes in place of manual checks.
